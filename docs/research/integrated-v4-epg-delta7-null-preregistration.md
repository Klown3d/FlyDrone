# V4 EPG→Delta7 projection-specific null preregistration

## Question

Does the intact FlyWire-grounded EPG→Delta7 partner arrangement outperform
20 rewired EPG→Delta7 matrices while Delta7→PFL3 remains exactly intact?

## Frozen design

Twenty new side-block rewiring seeds and one intact condition will be evaluated
on 12 new paired cases, totaling 252 simulated flights. Rewiring preserves
binary row and column degrees and every Delta7 row's exact nonzero raw-weight
multiset within EPG source-side blocks. Weighted column sums and exact partner
identities are not preserved.

Delta7→PFL3, PFN→hDeltaB, PFL2, sensors, engineering layers, plant and
perturbations remain identical. Every null receives one outcome-independent
algebraic global phase alignment to the intact composite transfer.

Arrival success is primary. Intact must reach at least 10/12. The corrected
empirical rank value is `(1 + nulls with success >= intact) / 21`; the gate
passes only at p <= 0.05. Cross-track and phase residuals are secondary.

All seeds, targets, initial headings and scenarios differ from the five-null
projection-separated development diagnostic and from the Delta7→PFL3
prospective gate. No flight outcome from this matrix is inspected before freeze.
